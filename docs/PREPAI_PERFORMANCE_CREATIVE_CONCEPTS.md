# PrepAI performance creative concepts

Audience: US women ages 20–40 interested in fitness, wellness, recovery, and privacy.\
Placement: TikTok, Instagram Reels, and Facebook Reels, 9:16, US English.\
Objective: generate testable creative hypotheses for watch time, engagement, installs, and CPI.

These are brand-created concepts, not testimonials. If an AI avatar is used, follow the platform's
current synthetic-media disclosure rules. Use licensed commercial audio, keep important text inside
platform safe zones, and validate every app capture against the release being advertised.

## Claims boundary

Approved themes:

- PrepAI is iPhone-first and does not require a PrepAI account.
- It can use authorized Apple Health data or manual records.
- It provides explainable movement, meal, and recovery guidance.
- Optional AI features run on-device; core health processing stays on the device.
- A one-time lifetime purchase option is available alongside the currently advertised offer.

Do not claim that nothing ever leaves the phone, that PrepAI diagnoses or medically clears someone,
that it guarantees an outcome, or that every purchase is subscription-free. Do not advertise
supplements, labs, symptom inference, Watch, Android, weight loss, or invented customer results.

## Concept catalog

### 1. Dashboard Funeral

**Format:** Funny talking head with prop comedy.\
**Hook, 0:00–0:02:** “Another fitness dashboard has passed away. Cause of death: 47 charts.”\
**Script:**

- 0:00–0:02 — Hook to camera while holding an absurd stack of printed charts.
- 0:02–0:05 — “It tracked everything. It answered nothing.”
- 0:05–0:09 — “The hard part was still deciding what to do today.”
- 0:09–0:14 — “PrepAI turns authorized Apple Health or manual context into explainable movement,
  meal, and recovery guidance.”
- 0:14–0:17 — “One useful plan. Fewer tabs.” On screen: “Try PrepAI on iPhone.”

**Visual direction:** Fitness avatar at a kitchen counter, handheld framing, chart pages falling out
of frame, then a real PrepAI Today capture in a Rotato phone mockup. Keep the performance dry and
slightly under-rehearsed.\
**Why test it:** A visual joke makes an abstract “dashboard overload” problem immediately legible,
then creates a clean problem-to-solution turn.\
**Caption:** “Respectfully, I did not need chart 48. #fitnessapps #wellnesstips #PrepAI”\
**Risk:** Safe.\
**Primary test variable:** Deadpan delivery versus exaggerated grief.\
**Required assets:** Talking avatar, paper-chart props, real Today capture, Rotato phone template.

**Visual direction:** One avatar composited into three cheap courtroom angles; obvious costume swaps,

**Why test it:** Familiar courtroom meme grammar creates conflict without making a medical or
performance claim.\
**Caption:** “The court recognizes walking as movement. ⚖️”\
**Risk:** Medium; keep “soreness” contextual and never imply injury clearance.\
**Primary test variable:** Courtroom hook versus “fitness police” hook.\
**Required assets:** Avatar green screen, three costume props, gavel SFX, PrepAI capture.

3. “Keep one easy meal made from foods you already enjoy.”
4. “Use a short walk as a real option—not a punishment.”

6. “Review what helped instead of only tracking misses.”
7. “Let today’s context change today’s plan.”
8. “PrepAI helps connect movement, meals, and recovery into one explainable next action.”

**Visual direction:** High-resolution attributed Pexels images mixed with one real PrepAI screen.
Use one type system, strong contrast, and 1.8–2.2 seconds per slide. The app promotion must be clearly

**Why test it:** Dense practical value supports saves and carousel completion while the product appears

**Caption:** “Save this for the week when motivation is not the problem—friction is.”\
**Risk:** Safe after content and stock-provenance review.\
**Primary test variable:** App mention on slide 7 versus slide 8.\
**Required assets:** Seven attributed portrait stock images, one PrepAI capture, carousel renderer.

## Measurement contract

Track each asset by concept ID, hook ID, platform, placement, publish date, spend, and app build shown.
Use the same body and CTA within a hook cell so the opening is the primary changed variable.

- Hook hold: two-second views divided by impressions.
- Six-second view rate.
- Completion rate.
- Saves, shares, and comments per view.
- Click-through rate.
- Install rate from attributed clicks.
- CPI: spend divided by attributed installs.

Safe and Medium concepts may enter paid testing after visual, claims, rights, and platform review.
Higher-risk concepts remain organic-only until comment sentiment and compliance are reviewed. Results
determine winners; the labels above are production priorities, not performance guarantees.
